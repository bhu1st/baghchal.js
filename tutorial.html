Lesson 1 of 5

1. Initial Board Setup

Baghchal is played on a 5×5 grid with 25 line intersections. The game begins with 4 Tigers (🐯) placed at the four corners of the board. The Goats start with 20 unplaced pieces in reserve.

3. Tiger Capture Rule (The Jump)

Lesson 4 of 5 • Goat Victory

4. Trapping Tigers (Goat Win)

Once all 20 goats are placed, goats can slide to adjacent connected points. The Goats win by completely surrounding all 4 Tigers so that no tiger has any legal move or jump available!

OBX Notation (All 4 Tigers Trapped):
TGGGT/GGGGG/GXGGG/GGGGG/TGGGT t @0 c0 mB3
Lesson 5 of 5 • Tiger Victory

5. Tiger Victory Condition

The Tigers win if they manage to capture (eat) 5 Goats before being trapped by the goats.

OBX Notation (5 Goats Captured):
TXXXT/XXGXX/XXXXX/XXXXX/TXXXT g @14 c5 mA1C3
